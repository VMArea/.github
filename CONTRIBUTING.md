# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve VMArea's open-source projects.

- **Bugs and ideas**: open an issue in the relevant repository. Include what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce.
- **Pull requests**: keep each one small and focused on a single change. Open an issue first for anything larger, so we can agree the approach before you spend time on it.
- **Tests and docs**: update them alongside the code they cover.
- **Licence**: contributions are accepted under the licence of the repository you are contributing to.
- **Security issues**: do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Everyone taking part is expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
